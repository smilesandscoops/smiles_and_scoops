Our Story

About Smiles & Scoops

A little bit about who we are and why we make what we make.

Made with Heart, Made by Hand

Smiles & Scoops began with a simple love for making things — a beaded bracelet for a friend, a crochet flower for a birthday card. What started as a hobby slowly grew into something we couldn't stop doing.

Today, every piece we make — jewelry or crochet flowers — is still handmade in small batches, checked over by hand, and packed with care before it reaches you.

We especially love custom requests. If you have a color palette, an occasion, or just an idea, send us a message — we'd love to make something just for you.
